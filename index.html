HTML5 Jigsaw Puzzle

Drag or pan to move pieces. (Moving multiple pieces work if you use touch.) Drag with right mouse button, with left mouse button and Ctrl, or pan with two fingers to move the viewport. Pin mouse wheel with Ctrl or pinch with two finger to zoom in or out the viewport. When neighboring pieces are close enough they snap to each other.

There are multiple images available, you can choose one from the sidebar or credits below. You can close your browser and continue to play later, or switch to other puzzle without destroying the progress; just make sure that you play on the same browser and computer (and keep the local storage).
